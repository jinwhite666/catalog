SECTION 1: THE SHIFT

AI-Powered Was the Feature. AI-Native Is the Architecture.

Most vendors bolted an AI feature onto a decade-old data plane. OEM buyers now ask a harder question: is intelligence a module you can remove, or the substrate everything else runs on? WhiteEdge is designed to answer that question at three deliberate maturity tiers — so partners can enter where their roadmap needs them, then graduate.

1. AI-POWERED

AI as an add-on capability inside an existing product — a feature, a widget, a model call (e.g. anomaly-detection dashboard bolted onto legacy NMS).

2. AI-DRIVEN

AI actively makes operational decisions — routing, remediation, policy — with a human approval loop (e.g. AI-recommended firewall policy change, human-approved).

3. AI-NATIVE

AI is co-designed into the data plane and control plane from first principles — the system reasons and acts in a closed loop (e.g. inference-in-path silicon driving autonomous policy).

SECTION 2: PRODUCT FAMILY

One Blueprint, Three Shippable Product Lines

Each line maps directly to an OEM SKU tier and can be sold standalone or converged.

AI-Native HW+SW closed-loop system. Silicon feeds reasoning plane in real time. Reasoning plane re-programs silicon behaviour. Sold as one OEM-branded reference design.

SECTION 3: HARDWARE BLUEPRINT

Silicon Appliance — Board-Level Architecture

  • Front Panel I/O: 48× 25GbE SFP28 + 8× 400GbE QSFP-DD + MGMT/Console.
  • Switching Fabric (ASIC): Non-blocking L2/L3 forwarding, hardware ACLs, MACsec.
  • Inference Silicon (NPU): Line-rate inference-in-path — traffic classification, anomaly scoring, policy scoring.
  • Secure Enclave: Hardware root of trust, secure boot, TPM 2.0, image signing.
  • Host CPU + Memory: Control-plane compute, telemetry buffering, ZTP/PnP agent.
  • Reference Spec Targets: 12.8 Tbps non-blocking, 400 Gbps line-rate @ <5µs latency, 1RU/2RU, <650W power.
SECTION 4: SOFTWARE BLUEPRINT

Reasoning Plane — Layered Architecture

  • Experience Layer: Web console, CLI, OEM-brandable UI shell, role-based dashboards.
  • API & Integration Layer: REST (OpenAPI 3.x), gRPC streaming, NETCONF/YANG, Webhooks, SSO/SCIM.
  • AI Reasoning Engine: Model orchestration, anomaly & intent scoring, policy recommendation, explainability trace.
  • Automation & Orchestration: Ansible/Terraform providers, workflow engine, human-in-the-loop gates.
  • Telemetry & Data Layer: Streaming ingestion (gNMI/syslog), time-series store, event bus.
  • Deployment: Deployable on-prem (VM/OVA), SaaS, or air-gapped.
SECTION 5: CONVERGED PLATFORM

The Closed Loop: What Makes It AI-Native

Silicon (Sense & Score) $\rightarrow$ Reasoning Plane (Correlate & Propose) $\rightarrow$ Policy & Control (Act & Re-program). Continuous re-tuning without forklift upgrades.

SECTION 6: DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Six Principles Every OEM Variant Must Preserve

  1. Inference-in-Path: AI scoring happens in the data plane.
  2. Human-in-the-Loop by Default: Autonomous action is opt-in and reversible.
  3. Composable Licensing: HW, SW, and converged variants share one BOM.
  4. Zero-Touch Security: Secure boot and hardware root of trust on every tier.
  5. Open Southbound / Northbound: Standard protocols in and out.
  6. Explainability as a Requirement: Every action carries an auditable trace.
