◆ Suggested 90-Day Sequencing

  1. Weeks 1–2: Register on Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent, and SecurityClearedJobs.com — these four alone cover both named focus sectors (GCC + Defense) and carry the highest signal-to-effort ratio.
