A project-based consulting marketplace connecting enterprises with independent consultants — largely ex-McKinsey, Bain, BCG, and Big Four — for multi-week to multi-month engagements, run more like contracting than a research call. Commission runs roughly 20–30% on client engagement value.

Engagement Strategy by Objective

Cross-mapping platform categories against what OMNIVEX/NexTier is actually trying to accomplish in each channel.

ObjectivePriority PlatformsWhy
Win GCC BFSI/Defense advisory engagements Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent Largest verified employer networks and salary benchmarking in the exact target geography.
Monetize UK DV clearance directly SecurityClearedJobs.com, ClearanceJobs, Electus Recruitment Purpose-built cleared-candidate pools; DV-specific filters put you ahead of generalist applicants.

◆ Suggested 90-Day Sequencing

  1. Weeks 1–2: Register on Bayt, Naukrigulf, GulfTalent, and SecurityClearedJobs.com — these four alone cover both named focus sectors (GCC + Defense) and carry the highest signal-to-effort ratio.
  2. Weeks 3–5: Build a Catalant and Business Talent Group profile positioning NexTier as an independent senior advisory practice — expect 1–2 week matching cycles, so start early relative to any pipeline deadline.
  3. Weeks 6–8: Layer in infosec-jobs.com and HackerOne presence to reinforce CRYSTAL AI-IPS technical credibility alongside the consulting narrative.
  4. Ongoing: Maintain LinkedIn and iimjobs.com as always-on visibility layers; treat Upwork/Toptal as opportunistic door-openers rather than core channels given NexTier's senior-advisory positioning.
