Solo Operator Profit Engine

The mechanics of how a solo consultant extracts maximum value from cybersecurity product ecosystems.

The SPM Framework

1
Choose a niche vendor — Partner with a specialist tool in GenAI Security, OT/ICS, or DSPM where few consultants exist. Scarcity = premium rates.
2
The tool does the discovery — Tools like Wiz, Claroty, and Tufin surface complex security debt automatically. You interpret and prescribe.
3
Bill for the outcome, not the hours — Charge a remediation retainer (12+ months) once the tool surfaces the problem. Software cost ~zero to you.
4
Stack services to compound margin — Layer Apex services (AI Governance) on top of Elite work (Cloud Remediation). Same client, multiple revenue streams.

Revenue Architecture

A
Door-opener (free assessment) — Regulatory Fine Exposure Calculator, SCADA Check, or Firewall Bloat Analysis. Costs you 2 hours, closes a 12-month retainer.
B
Project revenue — Architecture design, implementation roadmap. Fixed-fee. Typically $25K–$80K. Sets up the retainer.
C
Managed retainer (recurring) — Continuous cloud posture management, fractional SOC, compliance monitoring. $5K–$20K/month at 90%+ margin.
D
AI-leverage multiplier — Agentic AI (MS Security Copilot, SentinelOne Purple AI) lets you do the work of 3 analysts. Your time cost drops; billing stays the same.
