Corporate Identity & Leadership

White Enterprises Inc. is an AI-native technology firm helping large-scale operations and digital networks achieve total cognitive task automation and frictionless data processing. By deploying a self-orchestrating, AI-powered multi-agent software engine, we eliminate human workflow management overhead and manual engineering constraints.

The firm was founded and is led by Arvind Pardeshi (operating under the executive persona Jin White), serving as CEO & Principal Architect. Arvind is a Principal Security and AI Infrastructure Architect with over 16 years of global enterprise experience across the UK, US, and Australia, having driven initiatives at major organizations such as Infosys, CSS Corp, and NSC Global. He holds a UK Developed Vetting security clearance and specializes in AI-native security, algorithmic governance, and cloud-native infrastructure.

Under his leadership, our core engineering focuses on high-fidelity auditability, infrastructure decoupling, and the elimination of human integration latency at the perimeter edge through artificial intelligence. Structured as a Delaware C-Corporation with secondary operations in India, we are uniquely positioned to serve both US institutional markets and rapid-growth technical corridors in South Asia and the GCC.

Our Mission

To design, implement, and govern AI-powered, autonomous, self-remediating algorithmic frameworks capable of executing complex, high-throughput digital workflows natively at the infrastructure layer, ensuring absolute operational integrity, risk mitigation, and strict systemic precision.

Our Vision

The establishment of a comprehensively AI-automated, compliant enterprise computing ecosystem wherein critical operational mandates—including cross-jurisdictional data audits, database schema standardization, and multi-vendor security patch deployment—are continuously executed by interoperable artificial intelligence agents, thereby mitigating human processing constraints and ensuring continuous operational resilience.
