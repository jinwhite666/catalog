Purpose

Build a revenue-led certification stack that unlocks managed-service delivery (SASE, EDR/MDR, SIEM/SOC, cloud security) for BFSI and GCC clients across India, UAE and Singapore, sequenced by MSP revenue leverage.

Objectives

  • Clear free/low-cost entry certs first (Fortinet NSE 1–3, Zscaler ZCCA-IA/PA)
  • Complete the 15 ranked MSP priority certs
  • Convert each cert into a named revenue unlock
  • Hold daily study blocks without displacing outreach
  • After the sprint, climb the 20-cert Expert Track in four waves (architecture, cloud, AI security, offensive, expert network)

Scope

  • In: 15 priority certs, 12 sprint targets, 20 expert-track certs, 32 AI-focused certs, 158 vendor certs (33 vendors) as reference
  • Reference only: 481-cert level table and gross-margin mapping
  • Out: certs not on the priority list unless re-ranked

Success Criteria

  • Priority certs passed vs 15 target
  • Sprint milestones met across 4 phases
  • Each passed cert linked to a client conversation or service line
  • Renewals (2–3 yr) tracked for all earned certs

Constraints & Risks

  • Solo operator: study time shared with outreach and build work
  • Palo Alto exams: Pearson VUE in-person only; legacy PCNSE retired, use new role-based tracks
  • Some partner-only certs (ZDX-SP, EDU-302, Darktrace) need partner onboarding first
  • Expert certs carry experience prerequisites and endorsement; prices, codes and retirements change often, so verify on the vendor site before booking
  • Exam costs marked "Paid"/TBC are not counted in spend

Governance

  • Review: weekly status check; re-rank priorities monthly

Reference notes

How the lanes differ

  • AI security: technical defence and testing (SecAI+, GIAC, OffSec, CAISP)
  • Governance & risk: policy, audit, ISO 42001 (AIGP, AAISM, AAIA, AAIR)
  • Cloud AI engineering: building AI on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud
  • AI infrastructure: NVIDIA LLM and agentic AI
  • AI foundations: literacy and programme management (CompTIA AIE, PMI-CPMAI)

Watch-outs

  • Platform certs show depth on that platform, not general AI skill
  • GIAC/SANS is rolling out more AI certs through 2026; check the GIAC AI focus page for current availability
  • Microsoft AI-900 is retired; AI-901 is the current fundamentals exam
  • ISC2 AI security certification is announced, pilot exam expected late 2026; not yet bookable
  • Microsoft AI-103 replaces AI-102; some new Microsoft AI exams are in beta
  • ISACA AAISM, AAIA and AAIR require an existing qualifying credential

Suggested order for this plan

  • Now: SecAI+ and AIGP alongside the sprint
  • With the Expert Track: CISSP/CISM, then AAISM
  • Then: GAIPS, ISO 42001 Lead Implementer, AWS GenAI Professional
